# UI Craft & Brand Design System Skill

## 1. Anti-Generic Design Principles
- **Never build flat, uniform AI templates**: Avoid rows of 3 or 4 identical cards with the same icon-in-a-blue-circle pattern.
- **Asymmetric Bento Grids & Editorial Rhythm**: Combine wide feature cards (`col-span-7` or `col-span-8`) with vertical accent cards (`col-span-5` or `col-span-4`), interactive visual mockups, and tactile pill tags.
- **White & Blue Architectural Contrast**:
  - Primary Canvas: Crisp Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) alternating with Soft Alabaster/Ice (`#F4F7FB`) and subtle dot/grid architectural textures.
  - Anchor Accents: Deep Navy (`#07162E`) and Electric Cobalt (`#0866E8`) used for high-contrast focal cards, buttons, and interactive badges.
- **Multi-Color Expressive Iconography (Zero Unicode Text Emojis)**:
  - Use `@iconify/react` (`fluent-emoji-flat`, `solar:*-bold-duotone`, `logos`) and `@phosphor-icons/react` (`weight="duotone"`) instead of plain monochrome strokes or raw text emojis.
  - Pair each service/category with its own semantic color palette:
    - **Hardware / Mantenimiento**: Electric Blue & Cyan (`#0866E8` / `#06B6D4`)
    - **Asesoría Tecnológica**: Warm Amber & Orange (`#F59E0B` / `#EA580C`)
    - **Software & Web**: Vivid Violet & Indigo (`#7C3AED` / `#4F46E5`)
    - **Soporte & Celulares**: Emerald & Teal (`#10B981` / `#0D9488`)
    - **Audio & Accesorios**: Rose/Coral & Sky (`#F43F5E` / `#0EA5E9`)
- **Minimal Copy, Maximum Clarity**:
  - Visitors must understand what the business does within 3 seconds.
  - Product cards must be ultra-clean: high-contrast visual frame, product title, subtle quality badge, and a 1-click action.
